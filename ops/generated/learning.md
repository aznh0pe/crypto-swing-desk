Order of work for the day: ideas for today's market first, ideas for every market condition second, the backlog and the web after that.
   a. If spec_file is set, test it: `cd $C && python3 -m engine.research test --spec <spec_file> --data $W/data --state $W/state --out $W/rs1` (about a minute). Never lower the bar to fill a quiet day. Method: write the simplest version of a trade as a spec in $W/rs/base_spec.json (a universe, one or two conditions, an entry and an exit; no filters yet; the format is documented at the top of $C/engine/rules.py, and only the fields and functions listed there exist) and run `cd $C && python3 -m engine.research states --spec $W/rs/base_spec.json --data $W/data --out $W/st1`. A state that wins in one period only is noise: leave it. Serve the focus in its order: today's market, then every market condition, then conditions with no A+ strategy or a week behind its pace; the goal is more genuine A+ setups, never a lower bar. Win rate comes first when two ideas compete: prefer the higher win rate in both periods on more separate days, then the larger average trade. One of the two should start from something the desk measured recently (a lesson, a radar pattern in the latest close scan's "radar", a graded alert that surprised).
